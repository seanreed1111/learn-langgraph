# Regional Customs and Local Variations

The law of the Crown is the same everywhere, but each realm keeps customs of its own. Where this article conflicts with a royal decree, the decree controls; see [the hierarchy of authority](crown_law.md#the-hierarchy-in-brief).

## The North
<!-- jurisdiction: north -->

In the North the lord who passes a death sentence carries it out himself, with his own sword, and looks the condemned man in the eye. Oaths are sworn before a heart tree. [Guest right](guest_right.md#regional-variations) is held to be the strongest law of all. A foster-brother counts as kin, so killing him is [kinslaying](crimes_and_punishments.md#kinslaying). Bastards bear the surname Snow.

## The Riverlands
<!-- jurisdiction: riverlands -->

In the Riverlands the lord paramount holds court at Riverrun and hears disputes at the great fairs, where merchants bring their own cases. Because the Riverlands are the crossroads of the realm, [tolls](commerce_and_debt.md#tolls-and-customs) and contracts are heavily regulated. Guest right is applied mainly to guests who have shared a meal. Bastards bear the surname Rivers.

## The Vale
<!-- jurisdiction: vale -->

In the Vale the lord paramount judges from the Eyrie and imprisons the condemned in the sky cells, which have open sides. [Trial by combat](trial_by_combat.md) is more commonly demanded in the Vale than anywhere else. Bastards bear the surname Stone.

## The Reach
<!-- jurisdiction: reach -->

In the Reach the lord paramount holds court at Highgarden, and the law is administered with attention to rank and chivalry. [Trial by seven](trial_by_seven.md#present-standing) retains its last stronghold here, and knights of the Reach are quick to claim it. Bastards bear the surname Flowers.

## The Westerlands
<!-- jurisdiction: westerlands -->

In the Westerlands the lord paramount holds court at Casterly Rock. Debts are strictly enforced, and the lord's gold underwrites a stern code of [debt and contract](commerce_and_debt.md#debts-and-creditors). Bastards bear the surname Hill.

## The Stormlands
<!-- jurisdiction: stormlands -->

In the Stormlands the marcher lords hold justice swiftly, because the realm is often at war. Disputes over land are settled by [trial by combat](trial_by_combat.md#when-it-may-be-demanded) if the lord paramount cannot decide. Bastards bear the surname Storm.

## Dorne
<!-- jurisdiction: dorne -->

In Dorne the eldest child inherits regardless of sex, as described in [Dornish equal primogeniture](succession_and_inheritance.md#dornish-equal-primogeniture). A marriage may be dissolved by mutual consent before the Prince's magistrate; see [Dornish dissolution](marriage_and_family_law.md#dornish-dissolution). Bastards carry no stigma and bear the surname Sand.

## The Iron Islands
<!-- jurisdiction: ironislands -->

On the Iron Islands the law of the Drowned God holds sway. Guest right begins only after a salt oath. The eldest son inherits, but a lord who cannot hold his seat may be displaced by the captains' moot, as noted in [the Iron Islands](succession_and_inheritance.md#the-iron-islands). Bastards bear the surname Pyke.

## The Crownlands
<!-- jurisdiction: crownlands -->

In the Crownlands the king is the lord paramount, and justice is administered by royal bailiffs and the Justices of the Crown; see [royal justice in the Crownlands](crown_law.md#royal-justice-in-the-crownlands). Bastards are called Waters.
