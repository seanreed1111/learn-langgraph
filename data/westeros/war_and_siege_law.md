# The Laws of War and Siege

War between lords, and war against the Crown, is governed by custom that is older than the Iron Throne. Breaking these customs is a crime, and some breaches are treason.

## Declarations and Banners

A lord who goes to war must call his banners openly and send word to his enemy. A lord who attacks without declaring war commits an offense against the king's peace. A war against the Crown is [treason](treason_and_attainder.md#what-is-treason).

## Hostages and Ransom

A captured knight or lord may be held for ransom. The ransom is set by the captor and the captive's family, and is paid in gold. A hostage who has surrendered must be treated as a guest and kept from harm; see [guest right](guest_right.md#the-custom). A captor who kills a ransomed man commits [murder](crimes_and_punishments.md#murder).

## Surrender and Quarter

A knight who yields in battle and speaks the words of surrender must be spared. A castle that opens its gates when called to surrender must be spared sack. A castle that refuses terms and is taken by storm may be given no quarter. A captor who kills a man who has yielded is guilty of murder and is tried before the king or his lord paramount.

## Sack of Towns

A town that surrenders may not be sacked. A town taken by storm after refusing terms may be plundered for up to three days, after which the lord must restore order. A lord who allows a longer sack is guilty of an offense against the Crown.

## Treatment of Noncombatants

Women, children, septons, and maesters are noncombatants. Killing a noncombatant who has not taken up arms is murder, whatever the circumstances of the war. A lord whose men kill noncombatants must punish the offenders or answer for the crime himself.

## Violations by Lords

### Breaking the Peace

A lord who attacks under a flag of truce, or who kills a herald, breaks the king's peace. The penalty is death, and the offense is charged as [treason](treason_and_attainder.md#what-is-treason). A herald is protected by the same law that protects the [royal pardon](royal_mercy.md#heralds-and-proclamations).

### Killing a Guest in War

A lord who kills a guest or hostage under his roof breaks [guest right](guest_right.md#violations-and-sanctions) as well as the laws of war, and may be hanged without trial.
