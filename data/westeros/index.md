# Compendium of the Laws of the Seven Kingdoms

This compendium gathers the written laws and settled customs by which justice is done across the Seven Kingdoms. It is a work of fiction set in the world of Westeros and is organized by subject, with every article cross-referenced.

## Sources of Law

Law in the Seven Kingdoms has four sources. Royal decrees issued by the Iron Throne bind every realm. Edicts of the great lords bind their own lands. Ancient custom, preserved by maesters and septons, fills the gaps. Religious law governs the Faith and its servants. How these sources rank against one another is set out in [Precedence Between Crown and Lordly Law](crown_law.md#precedence-between-crown-and-lordly-law).

## A Note on Authority

When two articles appear to conflict, the more specific article controls, and a written royal decree controls over custom. See [the hierarchy of authority](crown_law.md#the-hierarchy-in-brief).
