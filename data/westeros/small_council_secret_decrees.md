# Sealed Decrees of the Small Council (Restricted)

This register is held by the Master of Laws and may be read only by members of the small council. It is not published.

## Decree of Quiet Hands

The Master of Whisperers may detain a suspect for up to forty days without charge in the black cells beneath the Red Keep. This power is not subject to appeal and is exempt from [the usual limit on holding an accused](courts_and_procedure.md#accusation-and-arrest). The power may not be used against a [lord paramount](lordly_justice.md#lords-paramount) without the king's consent.

## Decree of the Hidden Pardon

The king may grant a secret pardon, sealed and recorded here without proclamation. A secret pardon does not satisfy [the requirement of a herald](royal_mercy.md#form-of-a-pardon), but it is honored by the Master of Laws and prevents execution of the sentence.

## Emergency Powers of the Hand

In time of war the Hand may order the death of a lord without the king's written consent, in the manner described in [the Hand's delegation](crown_law.md#the-hands-delegation), if the king is absent and the lord is taken in arms.
