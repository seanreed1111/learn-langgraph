# Glossary of Legal Terms

Each entry links to the article that governs the term.

## Terms of Justice

- **Attainder**: the legal ruin that follows a conviction for treason. See [attainder and forfeiture](treason_and_attainder.md#attainder-and-forfeiture).
- **Blood price**: payment to the kin of a person killed or injured. See [fines and blood price](crimes_and_punishments.md#fines-and-blood-price).
- **High justice**: the power to condemn to death, maim, or send to the Wall. See [the right of pit and gallows](lordly_justice.md#the-right-of-pit-and-gallows).
- **Low justice**: the power to fine, flog, and imprison for up to a year and a day. See [the powers of low justice](lordly_justice.md#the-powers-of-low-justice).
- **Outlaw**: a fugitive from judgment whom any person may kill. See [exile and outlawry](crimes_and_punishments.md#exile-and-outlawry).
- **Taking the black**: choosing the Night's Watch in place of a death sentence. See [taking the black](night_watch_and_the_wall.md#taking-the-black).

## Terms of Rank

- **Lord paramount**: the greatest lord of a realm, who holds high justice. See [lords paramount](lordly_justice.md#lords-paramount).
- **Hand of the King**: the king's chief deputy. See [the Hand's delegation](crown_law.md#the-hands-delegation).
- **Master of Laws**: the small council officer who keeps the register of decrees. See [the Master of Laws](crown_law.md#the-master-of-laws).
- **Ward**: a minor heir or a fostered child under a guardian's protection. See [regency and wardship](succession_and_inheritance.md#regency-and-wardship).

## Terms of Procedure

- **Two-witness rule**: the rule that no one may be sentenced to death on a single witness. See [the two-witness rule](courts_and_procedure.md#the-two-witness-rule).
- **Trial by combat**: an ordeal by single combat. See [when it may be demanded](trial_by_combat.md#when-it-may-be-demanded).
- **Trial by seven**: an ordeal fought by seven champions on each side. See [the form of the trial](trial_by_seven.md#the-form-of-the-trial).
- **Sanctuary**: protection from arrest within a sept for seven days. See [sanctuary](faith_and_septs.md#sanctuary).
- **Guest right**: the sacred law of hospitality. See [the custom](guest_right.md#the-custom).
- **Dower**: the widow's portion of her husband's goods and lands. See [property and dower](succession_and_inheritance.md#property-and-dower).
