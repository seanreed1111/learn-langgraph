# The Crown's Law and the Machinery of Royal Justice

The king is the fountain of justice. All lawful authority to judge and punish is held in trust from the Iron Throne, and the king may take any case into his own hands.

## The King's Law

### Royal Decrees

A royal decree is a written command bearing the royal seal and proclaimed by heralds in the king's name. A decree takes effect in every realm on the day it is proclaimed in the capital, and lords must proclaim it in their own lands within one moon. A decree that is not sealed is not binding. Forging the royal seal is [treason](treason_and_attainder.md#what-is-treason).

### The Hand's Delegation

The Hand of the King speaks with the king's voice and may sit in judgment when the king is absent, ill, or a minor. The Hand may not issue a pardon, restore an attainted house, or order the death of a lord without the king's written consent. See [the royal pardon](royal_mercy.md#the-royal-pardon).

## The Master of Laws

The Master of Laws is a seat on the small council. The office keeps the register of decrees, advises the king on conflicts between statutes, and appoints the Justices of the Crown who ride circuit through the Crownlands twice each year to hear appeals and clear the cells. The Master of Laws has no power to try a lord.

## Precedence Between Crown and Lordly Law

### The Hierarchy in Brief

1. A sealed royal decree.
2. An edict of a lord paramount within the lord's own region.
3. Settled regional custom, as listed in [regional customs](regional_customs.md).
4. The edict of a lesser lord within the lord's own lands.

A lower authority may add to the law of a higher one but may never contradict it. A lord who sets aside a royal decree commits an offense against the Crown, and a repeated or open defiance may be charged as treason.

### Sacred Customs the Crown Will Not Override

By long usage the Crown does not override [guest right](guest_right.md), the sanctuary of the Faith described in [sanctuary](faith_and_septs.md#sanctuary), or the vows of the [Kingsguard](kingsguard_and_oaths.md). Kings who have tried have been condemned by lords and septons alike.

## Royal Justice in the Crownlands

In the Crownlands the king is also the lord paramount. Criminal cases there are tried by the royal bailiffs, with appeal to the Justices of the Crown. Elsewhere the king hears only appeals from the lords paramount and cases involving [treason](treason_and_attainder.md).
