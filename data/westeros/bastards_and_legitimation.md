# Bastards and Legitimation

A bastard is a child born outside marriage. The law gives a bastard fewer rights than a trueborn child, though the Crown may raise him.

## Status of a Bastard

### Surnames

A bastard does not bear his father's name. By custom each region gives its bastards a surname: Snow in the North, Rivers in the Riverlands, Hill in the Westerlands, Flowers in the Reach, Stone in the Vale, Storm in the Stormlands, Pyke on the Iron Islands, and Sand in Dorne. See [regional customs](regional_customs.md).

### Rights and Disabilities

A bastard may own property, hold office, and bear arms. A bastard may not inherit a lordship, lands, or titles from his father unless he is legitimized. A bastard has no claim in [succession](succession_and_inheritance.md#succession-to-lordships). In Dorne bastards carry no stigma and may be fully accepted by the family.

## Legitimation

### By Royal Decree

The king may legitimize a bastard by a sealed decree. A legitimized bastard takes his father's name and is entitled to inherit, but he ranks below all trueborn children of the same father in the line of succession. See [royal decrees](crown_law.md#royal-decrees).

### By Lordly Petition

A lord who wishes to legitimize his bastard must petition the king. The petition must state the father's name, the child's age, and the reason for the request. A petition is not valid if the father's trueborn heir objects and the king agrees.

## Bastards in Sworn Orders

A bastard may take the black and join the Night's Watch; see [who is accepted](night_watch_and_the_wall.md#who-is-accepted). A bastard who has been knighted may serve in the Kingsguard. A bastard who takes the black gives up all claim to inheritance, even if he is later legitimized.
