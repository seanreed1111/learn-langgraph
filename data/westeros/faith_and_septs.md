# The Faith, Sanctuary and Church Courts

The Faith of the Seven has its own laws, its own courts, and its own protections. The Crown honors them by long usage.

## Church Courts

Church courts have jurisdiction over septons, septas, and the sworn brothers of the Faith, and over matters of heresy and blasphemy. Church courts are presided over by a High Septon or by septons whom he appoints. A lord may not try a septon; see [limits on lordly power](lordly_justice.md#limits-on-lordly-power). Church courts also govern the rites of marriage; see [septon rites](marriage_and_family_law.md#septon-rites-and-northern-rites).

### Penalties

Church courts may impose penance, fines, and the loss of a septon's office. They may not impose death, but may hand a condemned person to the Crown. A person tried by a church court may not demand [trial by combat](trial_by_combat.md#when-it-may-be-demanded).

## Sanctuary

A fugitive who takes refuge in a sept may not be taken by force for seven days. During that time the septons may hear the fugitive and, if they find the charge serious, hand him to the lord who seeks him after a hearing. A person who breaks sanctuary by violence commits an offense against the gods and the Crown, and is charged under [crimes and punishments](crimes_and_punishments.md#homicide) if a death results. The Crown does not [override sanctuary](crown_law.md#sacred-customs-the-crown-will-not-override).

## The High Septon

The High Septon is the head of the Faith. He crowns the king, grants annulments under [annulment and dissolution](marriage_and_family_law.md#annulment-and-dissolution), and may speak for the Faith before the king. The High Septon is not subject to lordly justice.

## The Faith Militant

The Faith Militant was an armed order of sworn knights who enforced the Faith's law. The order was disbanded by the Crown after a long conflict, and no armed order of the Faith may now be raised without a royal decree. The Faith Militant's decline also reduced the use of [trial by seven](trial_by_seven.md#present-standing).

## Blasphemy

Blasphemy is speech or action that mocks the Seven. A person guilty of blasphemy may be fined and sentenced to penance. A second offense may be punished by flogging by the order of a church court.

## The Old Gods

In the North and in some other places the old gods of the First Men are still honored. An oath sworn before a heart tree is as binding as an oath sworn before a septon. See [oaths](courts_and_procedure.md#oaths) and [the North](regional_customs.md#the-north).
