# The Night's Watch and the Wall

The Night's Watch is a sworn brotherhood that guards the realm from the lands beyond the great Wall. It has its own commander, its own discipline, and a unique place in the law.

## Taking the Black

A person condemned to death for a lesser capital crime may choose to take the black instead of the sentence. The choice must be made before the sentence is carried out. The man swears the vows of the Watch, which bind him for life. His crimes before the vow are forgiven, his lands pass to his heirs, and he may not marry or hold land again. See the penalties in [crimes and punishments](crimes_and_punishments.md#the-black).

### The Vow

The vow is spoken before a heart tree or a septon and is a binding oath for life. A man who breaks it is a deserter. See [deserters](#deserters).

## Who Is Accepted

The Watch accepts most offenders: thieves, poachers, brawlers, and men who have committed lesser capital crimes. It does not accept:

- convicted rapists;
- unpardoned traitors, because a traitor condemned under [attainder](treason_and_attainder.md#attainder-and-forfeiture) cannot be forgiven by a vow alone;
- kinslayers, unless the Lord Commander agrees, as described in [kinslaying](crimes_and_punishments.md#kinslaying).

A younger son, a bastard, or any free man may also join voluntarily. See [bastards in the Watch](bastards_and_legitimation.md#bastards-in-sworn-orders).

## Jurisdiction of the Watch

Brothers of the Night's Watch answer only to the Lord Commander for offenses committed at or beyond the Wall. A lord may not try a sworn brother of the Watch; see [limits on lordly power](lordly_justice.md#limits-on-lordly-power).

## Deserters

A brother who leaves his post without leave is a deserter. The penalty for desertion is death by beheading, carried out by the person who captured him or, if the Lord Commander so orders, by the Lord Commander himself. A deserter who is caught in a lord's lands must be returned to the Wall. The Crown has no power to pardon a deserter against the Lord Commander's wish.

## Pardon by Service

A brother who has served with great merit may ask the king for a pardon for the crime for which he took the black. Such a pardon restores his name but not his lands, and does not release him from the vow. See [limits of the pardon](royal_mercy.md#limits-of-the-pardon).
