# Private Compacts Among the Lords on Blood Price (Noble Eyes Only)

This compact is held by the great houses and is not published to the smallfolk. It sets the amounts of blood price referred to in [fines and blood price](crimes_and_punishments.md#fines-and-blood-price).

## The Tariff

### Standard Amounts

- A freeman or smallfolk: 20 gold dragons.
- A landed knight: 100 gold dragons.
- A lord of a keep: 500 gold dragons.
- A lord paramount: 2,000 gold dragons.

### Adjustments

The amount is doubled if the killing was done in cold blood and halved if it was done in defense. A killing that breaks [guest right](guest_right.md#violations-and-sanctions) cannot be settled by blood price.

## Payment and Settlement

Payment is made to the victim's lord and to his nearest kin in equal shares. A house that refuses payment may take the matter to its lord paramount; see [lordly justice](lordly_justice.md#lords-paramount). A royal pardon does not cancel the debt; see [limits of the pardon](royal_mercy.md#limits-of-the-pardon).
