# Trial by Seven

The trial by seven is a rare ordeal in which seven champions fight on each side. It is held in the name of the Seven gods and has always been tied closely to the Faith.

## Origin

The trial by seven was practiced by sworn knights in the early centuries after the Andal invasion, when the Faith Militant policed the realm. It was meant for disputes among knights where the honor of a whole sworn order was at stake. See [the Faith Militant](faith_and_septs.md#the-faith-militant).

## The Form of the Trial

### The Parties

The accuser and the accused each name seven champions. The accused may fight as one of his own seven. Each side's champions are sworn knights.

### The Fight

The fight takes place before a septon, who calls on the Seven to judge between the sides. Arms and armor are chosen by the champions. The fight continues until one side has no champion left standing, or until one side yields. The side that stands victorious is held to be in the right.

## When It May Be Used

The trial by seven may be demanded only by a knight, and only in a case touching his honor or the honor of his order. It may be allowed in place of [trial by combat](trial_by_combat.md) by a lord or by the king in a capital case. The judge is not required to grant the request.

## Present Standing

Since the Faith Militant was disbanded as an armed order, the trial by seven has fallen out of favor. It remains lawful, but the Crown must grant permission in writing. A trial by seven held without royal permission is a breach of the king's peace and the champions may be charged under [crimes and punishments](crimes_and_punishments.md).
