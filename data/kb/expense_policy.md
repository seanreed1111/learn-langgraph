# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## What is reimbursable
Business travel, client meals, software subscriptions approved by your manager, and a one-time home office equipment stipend of 500 dollars for new remote employees.

## Limits and receipts
Meals are capped at 60 dollars per person per day while traveling. Every expense over 25 dollars needs an itemized receipt. Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase using the finance portal.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next bi-weekly payroll. Expenses over 1,000 dollars need director approval before purchase.
