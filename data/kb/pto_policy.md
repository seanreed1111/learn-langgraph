# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, which is 18 days per year. PTO accrues from the first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Days above the cap expire on January 31.

## Requesting time off
Request PTO in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for trips longer than 3 days. Your manager approves or declines within 2 business days. Sick leave is separate and does not use PTO.
