# Compensation Bands (CONFIDENTIAL)

Restricted to HR. Level L3 engineers earn a base salary between 110,000 and 135,000 dollars. Level L4 engineers earn between 135,000 and 165,000 dollars. Level L5 senior engineers earn between 165,000 and 205,000 dollars. Equity grants vary by level and are reviewed annually. Do not share these bands outside HR and the compensation committee.
