# Security Basics for Employees

## Passwords and authentication
Use the company password manager for every account. Passwords must be unique and at least 16 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on email, the VPN and all production systems. Hardware security keys are preferred over SMS codes.

## Reporting incidents
If you lose a device or suspect phishing, report it within one hour to the security team at the #security-help channel. Do not try to investigate on your own.

## Data handling
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Share files only through approved company tools.
