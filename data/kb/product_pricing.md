# Product Pricing and Plans

## Plans
The Starter plan costs 19 dollars per user per month and includes 5 projects and community support. The Team plan costs 49 dollars per user per month and adds unlimited projects, single sign-on and priority email support. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds audit logs, a dedicated success manager and a 99.9 percent uptime SLA.

## Billing
Annual billing gives a 20 percent discount. Plans can be upgraded at any time and are prorated. Refunds are available within 30 days of the first purchase.
