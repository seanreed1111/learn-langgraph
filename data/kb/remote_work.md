# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Most roles are remote-first. Employees may work from any country where the company has a legal entity. Working from another country for more than 30 days a year requires approval from HR for tax and legal reasons.

## Core hours
Everyone is available for collaboration between 10:00 and 15:00 in their own time zone. Outside those hours people work flexibly.

## Equipment
New hires receive a laptop shipped before day one and a 500 dollar one-time stipend for desk, monitor or chair. Replacement laptops are issued every 3 years.
